Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version

  • The Foxpace app keeps everything on the phone. We can't see it.
  • There's no account, no sign-in and no email address in the app.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no crash reports.
  • The app doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else.
  • This website sets no cookies and has no tracking.
  • If you email us or sign up for testing, we use your email address only for that.

The Foxpace app

What the app stores on the phone

To work, Foxpace saves a few things in its own storage on the phone. This data stays on the phone. The app doesn't send it to us or to anyone else, so we never receive it.

Mission titles are free text. Please don't type in anything you wouldn't want others to see, such as health details. See also “Notifications and the widget” below.

What the app doesn't collect

  • No account, email address or phone number.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos or files.
  • No microphone or camera. Foxpace has no access to them.
  • No advertising ID, analytics, usage statistics or crash reports from the app itself.
  • No chat and no way for anyone to contact a child through Foxpace.

Notifications and the widget

Reminders, focus checks and the end of a pause appear as notifications. The phone schedules them itself; they aren't sent from the internet. They can show the child's name and the mission title, and depending on the phone's settings they may appear on the lock screen. If you add the home-screen widget, it shows the next mission's title and time. Anyone who can see the phone can see these. You can turn notifications off or hide them on the lock screen in the phone's settings, and Foxpace still works.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders described above. You can say no.
  • Vibration: for reminders, if vibration is turned on.
  • Keep the screen on: during a mission, so the timer stays visible.
  • Run after a restart: so planned reminders still arrive after the phone is turned off and on.
  • Internet: Android lists this because it's included in standard building blocks the app uses. Foxpace itself doesn't send or receive your data.

Backups

On Android, Foxpace turns off the phone's automatic app backup, so its data isn't copied to your Google account. This also means the data doesn't move to a new phone. We'll update this section before the iPhone version is released.

App stores

When you download Foxpace from Google Play (and later from the App Store), Google or Apple handle the download and may collect data under their own privacy policies. They may give us anonymous, combined statistics, such as the number of installs. We don't receive personal information about you from them.

Children

Foxpace is made for children. The app doesn't ask for personal information beyond a name to greet them, and everything stays on the phone. Parents and carers can see and change everything in the app, and can delete it at any time. Everything else on this website, including emailing us and signing up for testing, is for adults only.

Deleting app data

  • Delete the app: everything Foxpace stored on the phone is removed.
  • Or, on Android: Settings › Apps › Foxpace › Storage › Clear storage. The exact menu names depend on the phone.
  • In the app, the name can be changed in Settings, and planned missions can be cancelled.

This website

Hosting

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technical data, including your IP address, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. GitHub may store this data in server logs; how long is set out in GitHub's privacy statement. We don't get access to these logs.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a website that works and is secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has issued an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising services, and loads no fonts or content from other providers. Its fonts are stored on the website itself.

If you choose English or German, your browser saves that choice on your device (in its local storage) so the page opens in that language next time. Nothing is sent to us. This is strictly necessary for the language choice you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). It stays until you clear your browser's website data.

Contacting us and signing up for testing

If you write to us by email or with the contact form, or asked earlier to be told when testing opens, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and your message. We use them only to reply, or to send you the test link. We don't send newsletters and don't pass your details on.

The legal basis is your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for general questions, our legitimate interest in answering them (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Giving your email address is voluntary, but we can't reply without it. Emails reach us through our email provider.

The contact form is run for us by Formward (EGF Group AB, Stockholm, Sweden), which acts only on our instructions under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR). Formward stores form messages on servers in Sweden, keeps IP addresses only in scrambled (hashed) form to stop spam, and deletes messages after 30 days. It sends us each message by email through Mailjet in France. Your data stays in the EU.

We delete your messages once your question is settled, and test sign-ups when testing ends or when you ask, whichever comes first.

Testers of the Android version join a Google Group (foxpace-testers) and then install the app from Google Play with the same Google account. Google runs the group under its own privacy policy. As the group's owner, we can see the email address of the account that joins; other members can't. We use it only to run the test. You can leave the group at any time, which also ends access to the test version. We'll remove the group when testing ends.

Your rights

  • have its use restricted (Art. 18);

  • object to its use where we rely on legitimate interest (Art. 21).

Write to hello@foxpace.de. Because the app keeps everything on the phone, we hold no app data about you.

Privacy policy

Last updated: Draft: publication date

Draft for review. This text describes how the Foxpace app works today. It is not legal advice and needs a legal check before it is published. Under EU law (GDPR) it will also need a German version.

The short version

  • Foxpace keeps everything on your phone. We can't see it.
  • There's no account, no sign-in and no email address.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no crash reports.
  • Foxpace doesn't send your data anywhere.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

What the app stores on your phone

To work, Foxpace saves a few things in its own storage on the phone. This data never leaves the phone through Foxpace.

WhatWhy
The name the child types inSo Foxpace can greet them. It can be changed in Settings at any time.
Missions: names, dates, times, length and focus-check intervalSo planned and saved missions are there when needed.
Finished and recent missionsFor the Recent Missions list and to show what's done.
The mission in progressSo a mission carries on if the app is closed or the phone restarts.
Settings: reminders, sounds, colours, text style, pause lengthsSo Foxpace behaves the way you chose.
Whether notification permission was already askedSo Foxpace doesn't ask again and again.

What we don't collect

  • No account, email address or phone number.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos or files.
  • No microphone or camera. Foxpace has no access to them.
  • No advertising ID, analytics, usage statistics or crash reports.
  • No chat and no way for anyone to contact a child through Foxpace.

Phone permissions

  • Notifications: reminders for planned missions and focus checks. The phone schedules them itself. They aren't sent from the internet. You can say no, and Foxpace still works.
  • Vibration: for reminders, if vibration is turned on.
  • Keep the screen on: during a mission, so the timer stays visible.
  • Run after a restart: so planned reminders still arrive after the phone is turned off and on.
  • Home-screen widget: if you add it, it shows the next mission on your home screen. Anyone who can see the phone can see that.
  • Internet: Android lists this because it's built into standard parts the app uses. Foxpace itself doesn't send or receive your data. Draft: confirm before publishing

Phone backups

If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include Foxpace's data in your Google account backup, like it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, not by Foxpace. Draft: decide whether to turn off backups in the app

App stores

When you download Foxpace from Google Play (and later from the App Store), Google or Apple handle the download and may collect data under their own privacy policies. We don't receive personal information about you from them. Draft: check the store reports before publishing

Joining the test

While Foxpace is in testing, families join through a Google Group, then install the app from Google Play. As the group's owner, we can see the email address of the Google account that joins. We use it only to run the test, we don't send newsletters, and we remove the group when testing ends. You can leave the group at any time, which also ends access to the test version. Waiting list for Foxpace for parents

If you sign up on the website, we keep your email address only to tell you when Foxpace for parents is ready. You'll first get an email asking you to confirm. Every email has a link to unsubscribe, and you can also write to us to be removed. We delete the list once we've sent the launch email. The list is kept with Brevo, a French email service, which stores it in the EU and acts only on our instructions. Draft: confirm with Brevo’s data processing agreement

Children

Foxpace is made for children. It doesn't ask children for personal information beyond a name to greet them, and that name stays on the phone. Parents and carers can see and change everything in the app, and can delete it at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Delete the app: everything Foxpace stored is removed from the phone.
  • Or, on Android: Settings › Apps › Foxpace › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Missions can also be cancelled one by one in the app, and the name can be changed in Settings.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Because Foxpace keeps everything on your phone, we hold no app data about you. If you contact us by email, we use your message only to reply and delete it when it's no longer needed.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. The responsible one is the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, datenschutz-berlin.de.

This website

This website sets no cookies and has no tracking or analytics. If you pick a language, your browser remembers that choice on your device so the page opens in it next time. Nothing is sent to us.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web server, GitHub records technical data when you open a page, including your IP address, to deliver the page and keep the service secure. We don't get access to these logs.
